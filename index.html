Day 1 • Creative Writing

Story in a Box Project

Collaborative Worldbuilding & Short Story Challenge

  • Goal: Build a shared narrative universe in teams of 3–4.
  • Day 1: Randomize universe elements & write your group hook.
  • Day 2: Write an individual scene with fun constraints.
  • Day 3: Revise, micro-conference, and share excerpts.

Use ⬅️/➡️ arrow keys or the controls below to navigate.

Day 1 • Shared Universe Launch

Group Formation & Rules

1. Team Up

Gather into groups of 3 or 4. Assign one driver to generate your prompt elements on the next slide.

2. The Challenge

You cannot swap prompts individually! You must accept the random elements drawn from the digital box and build around them together.

Day 1 • Wrap Up

Lock In Your Universe Hook

Collaborate with your team to finalize one complete, cohesive sentence based on your generated items:

“In [Setting], a character who [Quirk] must face [Conflict] after [Object] appears.”
  • Submit your finalized sentence to the teacher board before class ends.
  • Everyone in your group will write their own unique scene inside this same universe tomorrow!
Day 2 • Drafting Day

Warm-Up & Mini-Lesson

Warm-Up: 30-Second Cinematic

  • What does the scene look like?
  • What does it sound like?
  • What does the main character want right now?

Mini-Lesson: Show, Don't Tell

Instead of naming an emotion, describe the physical action or concrete detail that reveals it.

“Her fingers shredded the edge of the paper napkin into quiet confetti under the table.”

Day 2 • Individual Writing Task

Scene with a Twist Constraints

Write a 1–2 page scene connected to your group's universe. You must include:

  • 1 Concrete Setting Detail
  • 1 Drawn Object from your group box
  • 1 Dialogue Exchange (minimum 4 lines)
  • 1 Required Sentence Starter: Begins with “Of course,” or “I didn’t mean to…”
  • A Twist Ending: Something is not what it first seemed!
Day 2 • Peer Feedback

Glow & Grow Exchange

Swap drafts with a partner and complete the short feedback form:

🌟 Glow (Compliment)

“I really liked how you…”

🌱 Grow (Question)

“I was curious about…”

Day 3 • Revision & Showcase

10-Minute Revision Sprint & Reflection

Select ONE Revision Focus

  • Stronger twist / emotional shift ending
  • Clearer character motivation
  • More vivid sensory details
  • Realistic dialogue adjustments

Exit Reflection

Answer one prompt on your exit card:

• “Today I improved my writing by…”

• “My next writing goal is…”
